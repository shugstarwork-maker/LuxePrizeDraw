# Luxe Challenge Lounge — Terms & Rules (Draft v0.1 for legal review)

**Status: DRAFT — not legally reviewed. Hand to counsel before any public use.**
Operator: LuxePrizeDraw Ltd (England & Wales). Pre-launch; no paid entries, no prizes awarded.

## 1. What the Challenge Lounge is
A free-to-play skill-games arena (Luxe Chess, Draughts, Connect Four) operated by LuxePrizeDraw Ltd. All games are 100% games of skill with no random elements. Outcomes are decided entirely by player moves.

## 2. Eligibility
- 18+ and resident in the United Kingdom.
- One account per person, enforced via device fingerprint, unique phone number (SMS OTP) and ID verification before any prize play.
- Employees of LuxePrizeDraw Ltd and their households are excluded.

## 3. Virtual currencies
- **Gold Coins**: for-fun currency. Zero real-world value; cannot be exchanged for cash, prizes, tickets or points. Purchasable in bundles; card purchases capped at £250 per calendar month, and at any lower personal limit the player sets.
- **Sweep Tickets**: prize-entry currency. **Never sold.** Obtained only free of charge via (a) daily claim, (b) free bonus included with Gold Coin bundles, (c) postal entry (see §5).
- **Prize Points**: won only by winning prize matches. Redeemable in the Prize Store after launch.

## 4. No purchase necessary
Entry into any prize match or draw never requires a purchase. Free entry routes are presented with equal prominence to any paid option.

## 5. Postal entry (AMOE)
A player may send their full name and account ID on a postcard to the operator's registered UK business address. Each valid letter is credited with 5 free Sweep Tickets (maximum one credit per account per week). Postal entries carry identical odds and rights to every other entry route. Credits are logged with the letter reference for audit.

## 6. Match rules
- Per-player clocks: Chess 10 min, Draughts 5 min, Connect Four 2 min. Flag-fall loses.
- Disconnect grace: 60 seconds to reconnect; afterwards the match is forfeited and any entry passes to the opponent.
- If no opponent is found within 30 seconds, the matchmaking attempt is cancelled and any ticket entry is automatically refunded in full.

## 7. Fair play and anti-cheat
- Engine assistance, bots, scripted input, collusion and multi-accounting are prohibited.
- Move accuracy, move-time distribution and app-focus telemetry are recorded for every prize match.
- Combined risk score ≥75 pauses the match for review. Escalation: Tier 1 shadow flag → Tier 2 match kill (entry refunded to the non-flagged player) → Tier 3 hardware ban (device, payout route and IP range blacklisted).
- Players at the same IP address or network, or with repeated historic pairings, may be barred from the same prize table.
- Decisions on confirmed engine assistance are final, subject to the complaints route in §11.

## 8. Prizes and fulfilment
- Prize values and store contents are published before any prize play opens.
- Voucher redemptions over £500 are held for a 24-hour manual review (AML), including KYC and match-log checks, before fulfilment.
- Minimum withdrawal £20; maximum £500 per day. Payouts go only to a bank account or PayPal in the exact verified name.

## 9. Safer play
- Reality-check reminders at the player's chosen interval (30/60/120 minutes).
- Cooling-off (24h / 7 days) and self-exclusion (30 days / 6 months / permanent) available at any time and irreversible until expiry.
- Personal monthly spend limits always override and sit below the £250 legal ceiling.
- Signposting to GamCare, BeGambleAware and GAMSTOP is maintained in-app at all times.

## 10. Data
Account, verification, telemetry and ledger data are processed under UK GDPR. Server infrastructure is UK-hosted (eu-west-2). Records relevant to draws and payouts are retained for audit.

## 11. Complaints
In-app support first; unresolved disputes may be escalated per the final published complaints procedure. (To be completed with counsel.)

## 12. Amendments
Material rule changes are published in-app before taking effect and recorded in the draw history.
